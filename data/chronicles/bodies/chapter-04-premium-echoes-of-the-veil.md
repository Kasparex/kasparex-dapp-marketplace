# Echoes of the Veil

The rooftop arrays pulsed with a rhythm that was almost musical - if you could call static a song.

Krex stood where the wind bit hardest, coat snapping like a flag no army had earned. Below, Kaspaland stretched in layers of neon and omission: whole blocks scrubbed from maps, their absence stitched over with cheerful placeholders. The Null Gang did not destroy only buildings. They deleted *belief* in what had been there.

> "They don't want witnesses," Tessa had said hours earlier, voice flat over encrypted relay. "They want *forgetting*. We're fighting erosion."

Vector's overlay still ghosted along Krex's periphery - diagnostics, thermal ghosts, the faint amber thread that meant **someone** was mirroring their feed from inside the Veil. Not intrusion. *Echo*.

## What stays when the code goes quiet

Sarah's old routing signatures had resurfaced in a shard dump nobody was supposed to keep. Not proof of life - proof of *pattern*. The Silent Protocol had taught them that some ghosts were documentation errors. Others were warnings folded into whitespace.

Krex opened the vault line to the Diamond Veins relay - temporary bridge, burn-after-handshake - and pushed a single packet: coordinates that didn't exist on public chains, a wink to allies who knew how to read negative space.

The city inhaled.
