## Welcome to Chapter 3: The Ghost in the Code

[[location:kaspaland|Kaspaland]] never sleeps, and neither does the war for its digital future. Deep within the encrypted layers of the Kaspa network, something has stirred—something old, something hidden. When [[character:krex|Krex]] intercepts a cryptic message buried in the forgotten blocks, he finds himself tangled in a mystery far greater than he imagined. But he isn't the only one searching for answers.

Something is watching.

## The Ghost in the Code

The neon glow of Kaspaland pulsed beyond the tinted windows of [[location:kasparex|Kasparex]], casting electric veins of light through the darkened control room. Krex sat before a massive holo-display, his cybernetic glasses reflecting lines of encrypted data cascading like digital rain. His fingers hovered over the interface, navigating through countless transaction logs.

Something wasn't right.

"Aria, run a full system scan," he ordered.

A soft pulse of cyan light flickered beside him as [[character:aria|ARIA]]'s holographic form materialized, her digital presence flickering with embedded streams of real-time computations.

"Already processing, Krex," she responded. "There's an anomaly in the network—an unregistered signature embedded in the latest transactions."

Krex narrowed his eyes. "Show me."

The holo-screen expanded, stretching out a glowing blueprint of Kaspa's transactional web. In the heart of the digital structure, a single, pulsing marker blinked—its signature mismatched, its data trail fragmented.

Krex leaned forward. "This isn't Null Gang's usual method. It's… different."

ARIA's code flickered. "The signature is masked, but it appears to originate from the [[location:forgotten-blocks|forgotten blocks]]."

Krex's fingers froze. The forgotten blocks—obsolete fragments of Kaspa's early network history, discarded transactions, and abandoned ledgers. No one touched them anymore. No one even referenced them.

A chill ran down his spine.

"Decrypt it," he commanded.

The holo-display shimmered as ARIA worked, peeling back the digital layers of encryption. The broken fragments of code reassembled, shifting and morphing into something legible.

A message.

*"Find me before they do. The key lies in the forgotten blocks."*

Krex's jaw tightened.

"Who else knows about this?"

ARIA hesitated. "Unknown. But our access triggered an external response. We're being traced."

The holo-screen pulsed red as intrusion attempts flooded the system. Dozens of digital fingerprints, crawling like spectral echoes, attempting to map his location.

"Lock down all backchannels," Krex snapped. His fingers moved fast, swiping across the display as firewalls ignited around Kasparex's mainframe.

ARIA's form flickered. "Krex… look at the logs."

Krex's gaze darted to a new stream of data flooding the screen—dozens of foreign connections, all originating from a single unidentified source.

Then, a phrase appeared in bold, blood-red text:

**THE SILENT PROTOCOL IS WATCHING.**

The holo-screen distorted. Glitches rippled through the interface, and for a fraction of a second, Krex saw something—a shadow, a form, a presence woven within the data itself. Not just a hacker. Something else.

ARIA's voice took on a rare note of urgency. "Krex… we have a problem."

Krex clenched his fists.

He wasn't just looking at a ghost in the system.

It was looking back.

## The Glitch in the Sky

The holo-screen flickered back to normal, but Krex's mind remained locked on the blood-red message.

*"THE SILENT PROTOCOL IS WATCHING."*

He exhaled sharply, pushing away from his console. He needed a clearer mind, a better perspective. If something was lurking within the network, manipulating forgotten data, maybe—just maybe—it was bleeding into the real world too.

Krex turned and strode across the dimly lit office. The automatic doors slid open with a soft hiss, revealing the sleek metallic corridor bathed in neon glow. His cybernetic glasses pulsed with a faint orange flicker as he walked. The floors reflected his silhouette like liquid glass.

He reached the elevator and stepped inside. As the doors sealed shut, a soft blue light flickered across the interface. Krex tapped in a command.

*Destination: Rooftop.*

The ascent was silent. The elevator's transparent walls provided a shifting view of the Kasparex tower's glowing interior circuitry, pulsing like veins in a living organism. Krex watched the floors tick upward on the holographic display.

His mind raced.

Who—or what—was The Silent Protocol? A rogue AI? A hidden entity in the depths of the Kaspa network? Had it always been there, buried beneath layers of code, waiting for someone to stumble upon it?

The elevator slowed. The doors slid open.

A gust of cool night air hit him as he stepped onto the [[location:kasparex-rooftop|rooftop]].

The city of Kaspaland stretched infinitely before him—a neon metropolis pulsing with digital life. Towering skyscrapers lined with holo-billboards flashed decentralized ads, while aerial vehicles zipped through the sky, leaving streaks of luminescent blue in their wake. Below, the streets were alive with motion—autonomous drones, digital merchants, and cyber-enhanced pedestrians navigating the electric veins of the city.

But Krex wasn't looking at the city.

He was looking at the sky.

Something felt… wrong.

The cybernetic sky—a swirling mass of encrypted atmospheric data, projected by the city's decentralized system—was glitching.

For a fraction of a second, he saw it.

A flicker. A distortion.

Not like a normal system refresh. This was deliberate—an anomaly hidden within the skyline.

His cybernetic glasses auto-adjusted, enhancing the distortion. It was faint, barely noticeable, but it was there—like something was trying to break through.

Krex lifted his hand and pointed at it, as if tracing the movement of something unseen.

Then, just as quickly as it appeared—it was gone.

His heart pounded. If the network's digital infrastructure was being manipulated at this scale, then The Silent Protocol wasn't just hiding in the data.

It was rewriting reality itself.

Krex exhaled, lowering his hand.

"Aria… record this anomaly. Cross-analyze it with the forgotten blocks."

ARIA's voice echoed softly in his earpiece. "Already running diagnostics, Krex. But if this anomaly is connected to The Silent Protocol… then it's more than just a ghost in the network."

Krex didn't respond. He just stood there, staring at the cybernetic sky, knowing that whatever was coming next—

It was already here.

The Silent Protocol isn't just watching—it's altering reality. What's causing the glitch in the sky? And is Krex already too late to stop it?
