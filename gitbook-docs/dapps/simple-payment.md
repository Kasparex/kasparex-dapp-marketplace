## What is Simple Payment?

Send KAS payments with automatic fee collection to support the Kasparex ecosystem.

## How It Works

Enter recipient address and amount. A small fee (1%) is automatically deducted and sent to the treasury.

## Benefits

Simple, secure payments with transparent fee structure. Supports platform development.
