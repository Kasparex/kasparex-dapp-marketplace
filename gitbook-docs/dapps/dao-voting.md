## What is DAO Voting?

Submit and vote on future dApp ideas for marketplace integration

## How It Works

Submit proposals with 10 KAS fee, vote with 1 KAS fee per vote. High-vote proposals are flagged for review.

## Benefits

Community-driven dApp discovery and prioritization. Transparent on-chain voting with fee collection.
