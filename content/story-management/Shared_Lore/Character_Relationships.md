# Character Relationships – Kasparex Chronicles

This document tracks alliances, rivalries, and connections between all characters.  
Update it whenever new relationships form or evolve.

---

## Krex
- **Allies**: TORQ (loyal friend), Tessa (hacker ally), Vector (engineer ally), ARIA (AI assistant).  
- **Rivals**: Null Gang.  
- **Uncertain**: Sarah (unknown motives), Axel Vane (investor with unclear intentions).  

## TORQ
- **Bond**: Deep loyalty to Krex, comic relief + protector.  
- **Friends**: Gets along with everyone in the crew (Tessa, Vector).  
- **Enemies**: Clashes with Null Gang muscle.  
- **Dynamic**: Comic relief, but flips to serious protector when Krex is threatened.  

## Sarah
- **Connection**: Tied to Krex’s past, possibly manipulating events.  
- **Allies**: Unknown, appears alone.  
- **Enemies**: Might oppose Null Gang, but unclear if aligned with Krex.  
- **Mystery**: Her true motives remain hidden.  

## Vector
- **Bond**: Loyal to Krex, trusted with tech and infrastructure.  
- **Relationship**: Friendly rivalry with Tessa (hacker vs. engineer dynamic).  
- **Enemies**: Null Gang hackers.  

## Tessa
- **Bond**: Protects Krex with infiltration and cyber-defense skills.  
- **Friendship**: Close with Vector, strong trust in Krex.  
- **Enemies**: Null Gang infiltrators and cybercriminals.  

## ARIA
- **Connection**: Created by Krex as an AI ally.  
- **Relationship**: Supports entire crew, advisor role.  
- **Enemies**: Opposes Null Gang’s digital intrusions.  

## Null Gang
- **Rivals**: Krex and his entire team.  
- **Internal**: Chaotic alliances, not always loyal to each other.  
- **Enemies**: Potentially Sarah (if she’s not aligned).  

## Axel Vane
- **Connection**: Investor tied to Krex, motives unclear.  
- **Relationship**: Could fund or sabotage projects depending on his hidden agenda.  
- **Mystery**: Ally or foe? Still undecided.
