# Shared Utilities, Perks & Benefits – Kasparex Ecosystem

This file lists universal utilities, perks, and benefits across tokens.  
Update whenever a new perk is added.

---

## KREX (Core Token)
- Discounts on TORQ mini-games and raffles.
- Early unlock on Time-Locked Content Drops.
- Priority access in Collab Signer dApp.
- Premium Vector Toolkit modules unlocked.
- Airdrops of SARAH to KREX holders.
- Governance rights for future Kasparex expansions.

## TORQ
- Access to humor-driven games and raffles.
- Side Perk: TORQ holders receive exclusive SARAH-themed comic NFTs.
- Discounted NFT upgrades when paired with KREX.

## SARAH
- Unlocks SARAH Widget with cryptic utilities.
- Side Perk: Extra stealth modules in Vector’s Toolkit when holding both SARAH + VECTOR.
- Future perk: Unlock secret story chapters.

## VECTOR
- Grants access to modular developer dApps.
- Side Perk: If holding KREX, unlock premium modules.
- Side Perk: If holding SARAH, gain stealth enhancements.

## TESSA (Future)
- Cyber-defense perks, infiltration utilities.
- Discounted collab agreements when combined with KREX.

---

## Notes
- **KREX is always the central token** for discounts, early unlocks, and airdrops.  
- Other tokens can connect sideways, but only as **add-ons** to KREX’s ecosystem power.
