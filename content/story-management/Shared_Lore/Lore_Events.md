# Lore Events – Kasparex Chronicles

This document tracks important battles, discoveries, and turning points in detail.  
Unlike the Master_Timeline (broad eras), this file focuses on specific **events**.

---

## Early Era Events
- **The Creation of KREX Token**  
  Krex launches KREX on Kaspa KRC-20, granting power and wealth to holders.  
  Sparks curiosity and attracts allies.  

- **Founding of Kasparex HQ**  
  Krex establishes a skyscraper hub as his base of operations.  
  Acts as the nexus of story and ecosystem.  

---

## Rise of Conflict
- **First Encounter with Null Gang**  
  Krex and his team clash with the Null Gang in the Undercity.  
  TORQ proves his loyalty in a street battle.  

- **Appearance of Sarah**  
  A mysterious figure interrupts a Kaspa data-stream.  
  Her motives remain unclear, creating tension with Krex.  

- **Vector’s First Invention**  
  Vector unveils the first modular dApp toolkit prototype, boosting Kasparex’s capabilities.  

---

## Expansion Phase
- **The BlockDag Palace Meeting**  
  Secret meeting between Krex, Tessa, and hidden allies.  
  Potential betrayal foreshadowed.  

- **ARIA Activation**  
  Krex finalizes ARIA, an advanced AI assistant to help defend the network.  

- **Sarah’s Digital Intrusion**  
  Sarah hacks into the KREXPRIME NFT collection, leaving cryptic clues.  

---

## The Shift (Major Event)
- **Migration from KRC-20 to Smart Contracts**  
  A massive turning point in the ecosystem.  
  Old tokens migrate, unclaimed supply partially burned and reinvested.  

- **Null Gang’s Counterattack**  
  Attempt to corrupt migration process, repelled by Krex’s crew.
