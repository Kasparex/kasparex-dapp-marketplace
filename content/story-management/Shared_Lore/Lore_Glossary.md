# Lore Glossary – Kasparex Chronicles

This glossary defines important terms, locations, groups, and concepts in the Kasparex universe.  
Keep it updated as the story expands.

---

## Key Terms

### Kasparex
A central hub built by Krex, combining database, dApp marketplace, NFT utilities, and community features. The foundation of the ecosystem.

### Kaspaland
The futuristic, neon-lit world where Krex’s Chronicles unfold. Divided into districts (financial, underground, digital zones) with hidden layers of cryptography.

### BlockDAG
Kaspa’s underlying technology, enabling instant confirmations and parallel block creation. Powers all tokens and dApps in this universe.

### KRC-20 / Smart Contracts
Token standards within Kaspa. Many characters launch tokens first as KRC-20, later evolving into smart contracts.

---

## Characters & Factions

### Krex
Half-human, half-reptile, founder of Kasparex and creator of $KREX. Strategist, cryptography master, and main protector of the Kaspa network.

### TORQ
Krex’s loyal, funny, and strong sidekick. Comic relief with hidden powerhouse strength. Future token: $TORQ.

### Sarah
Mysterious figure, both ally and wildcard. Token: $SARAH, with cryptic tokenomics and perks for $KREX holders.

### Vector
Tech engineer and gadgeteer. Powers infrastructure, dApps, and NFTs. Token: $VECTOR.

### Tessa
Elite hacker and infiltrator. Loyal to Krex, skilled in cyber defense. Potential future token project.

### ARIA
AI assistant created by Krex to manage Kaspa network, guide strategy, and fight digital threats.

### Null Gang
Group of cybercriminals aiming to destabilize Kaspa. Antagonists of the story.

### Axel Vane
Mysterious investor connected to Krex. Motives unclear, could be ally or antagonist.

## Locations

### Kasparex HQ
Krex’s main base of operations. Features the Skydeck Office and rooftop overlooking Kaspaland.

### BlockDag Palace Club
Exclusive nightclub where deals, betrayals, and alliances happen.

### Undercity
The neon-slum layer of Kaspaland, home to hackers, gangs, and street hustlers like TORQ.
