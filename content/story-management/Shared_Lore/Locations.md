# Locations – Kaspaland Worldbuilding

This document details the main locations of Krex’s Chronicles.  
Update as new areas of Kaspaland are revealed.

---

## Kasparex HQ
- **Description**: Central base of Krex, futuristic skyscraper hub of operations.  
- **Features**: Skydeck Office, rooftop overlooking Kaspaland, cryptographic database core.  
- **Role**: Strategic heart of the Kasparex ecosystem.  

## BlockDag Palace Club
- **Description**: Elite nightclub in Kaspaland.  
- **Features**: Neon-lit halls, secret backrooms for deals, guarded VIP areas.  
- **Role**: Where alliances, betrayals, and secret negotiations take place.  

## The Undercity
- **Description**: Neon-drenched slums beneath the bright upper city.  
- **Features**: Gang hideouts, black markets, hacker dens.  
- **Role**: Base of Null Gang and street characters like TORQ.  

## The Neon District
- **Description**: Entertainment hub of Kaspaland.  
- **Features**: Arcades, VR lounges, casinos, street performers.  
- **Role**: Cultural melting pot, often a setting for TORQ’s antics.  

## The Data Spires
- **Description**: Towering cryptographic pillars storing Kaspa’s secrets.  
- **Features**: Constantly glowing with streams of encrypted data.  
- **Role**: Key to unlocking deeper lore and hidden technologies.  

## The Dark Grid
- **Description**: A shadow network outside normal Kaspaland.  
- **Features**: Illegal dApps, rogue AI, and ghost signals.  
- **Role**: Dangerous digital zone where Sarah and Null Gang operate.  

## Axel Vane’s Tower (Speculative)
- **Description**: Luxurious skyscraper of the mysterious investor.  
- **Features**: Hidden vaults, encrypted offices, unknown labs.  
- **Role**: Possible nexus of influence, ally or rival HQ.  

## Other Potential Locations
- **Kaspa Core Archives** – ancient repository of cryptographic keys.  
- **Skyrail Network** – high-speed transport system connecting districts.  
- **Cyber Bazaars** – markets for NFTs, tokens, and underground code.
