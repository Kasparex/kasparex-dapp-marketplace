# Factions & Organizations – Kasparex Chronicles

This document outlines major groups, factions, and organizations in Kaspaland.  
Each faction can later expand into its own arc, token, or NFT project.

---

## Kasparex
- **Leader**: Krex  
- **Members**: TORQ, Tessa, Vector, ARIA  
- **Goal**: Protect the Kaspa network, expand dApps, maintain decentralization.  
- **Base**: Kasparex HQ (Skydeck Office, rooftop, database center).  

## Null Gang
- **Type**: Cybercriminal syndicate  
- **Members**: Various hackers, mercenaries, rogue coders.  
- **Goal**: Destabilize the Kaspa network for personal gain.  
- **Base**: The Undercity, shifting hideouts.  
- **Notes**: Rivalry with Krex and his allies, possibly clashing with Sarah too.  

## Sarah’s Shadow Network (Unconfirmed)
- **Leader**: Sarah  
- **Members**: Unknown, possibly operates solo or through digital phantoms.  
- **Goal**: Hidden motives, appears to manipulate events from the shadows.  
- **Notes**: May either oppose or secretly support Kasparex depending on her arc.  

## Vector’s Workshop
- **Leader**: Vector  
- **Function**: Tech lab, responsible for gadgets, infrastructure, and NFTs.  
- **Notes**: Semi-independent but aligned with Krex. Potential to grow into DAO-like collective.  

## Tessa’s Infiltration Unit
- **Leader**: Tessa  
- **Function**: Stealth, hacking, infiltration operations.  
- **Notes**: Operates in both Kasparex HQ and Undercity. Handles cyber defense against Null Gang.  

## Axel Vane’s Syndicate (Speculative)
- **Leader**: Axel Vane  
- **Function**: Investments, hidden financial moves, secret ties.  
- **Goal**: Unknown, possibly manipulates multiple factions for his own ends.  

## Other Potential Factions
- **Kaspa Core Guardians** – defenders of ancient cryptographic secrets.  
- **Undercity Guilds** – loose gangs, merchants, and hustlers.  
- **BlockDag Palace Elites** – powerful figures in Kaspaland’s nightlife and finance.
