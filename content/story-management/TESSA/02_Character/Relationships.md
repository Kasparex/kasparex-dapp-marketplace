# Relationships for TESSA

## Krex
TESSA views Krex as a leader who understands the balance between power and responsibility. Their relationship is built on mutual respect and trust in execution rather than words.

## Vector
TESSA and Vector form a highly effective technical duo. While Vector builds and optimizes hardware and tools, TESSA ensures those systems are secure, resilient, and unexploitable.

## ARIA
TESSA maintains a professional, cautious relationship with ARIA. While she respects ARIA’s intelligence, she constantly audits AI decisions to ensure no unintended dominance or systemic bias emerges.

## TORQ
Though very different in personality, TESSA tolerates TORQ’s chaotic humor. She often acts as a grounding force when his actions risk exposure.

## SARAH
TESSA does not fully trust SARAH. She monitors her signals closely, unsure whether SARAH represents an ally, a threat, or something outside conventional alignment.

## Null Gang
The Null Gang considers TESSA a priority obstacle. Several failed operations have been traced back to her silent interference, earning her a hidden bounty within their circles.
