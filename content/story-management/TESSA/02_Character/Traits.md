# Traits for TESSA

- **Highly Analytical** – TESSA processes situations logically, rarely acting on impulse.
- **Calm Under Pressure** – Even during active breaches or network collapse events, she remains composed.
- **Selective Trust** – Loyalty is earned, not assumed.
- **Ethically Driven Hacker** – She refuses to exploit systems for personal gain alone.
- **Low-Profile Presence** – Prefers to operate behind the scenes rather than take credit.
- **Adaptive Thinker** – Adjusts strategies in real time as threats evolve.
- **Quietly Defiant** – Will challenge decisions if they compromise network integrity or values.
