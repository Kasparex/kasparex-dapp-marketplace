# Abilities for TESSA

## Deep Network Infiltration
TESSA can penetrate layered security systems without triggering alarms, allowing her to map hostile infrastructure before engagement.

## Counter-Surveillance
She specializes in detecting tracking, backdoors, and signal leaks, often neutralizing threats before they are noticed.

## Live System Patching
During active attacks, TESSA can deploy rapid countermeasures, isolating corrupted nodes while keeping the rest of the system operational.

## Signal Hijacking
She can intercept, reroute, or mask hostile transmissions, a critical skill against the Null Gang’s decentralized operations.

## Ghost Protocol
A personal failsafe method that erases her digital footprint mid-operation, making tracebacks virtually impossible.

## Tactical Data Support
Provides real-time intelligence to Krex, Vector, and ARIA during missions, enhancing decision-making and response timing.
