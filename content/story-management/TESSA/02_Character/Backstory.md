# Backstory for TESSA

TESSA grew up in the lower layers of Kaspaland, where data flowed faster than law and survival depended on knowing which systems to trust and which to break. From an early age, she learned to navigate encrypted networks not out of curiosity, but necessity.

Before meeting Krex, TESSA operated as an independent hacker-for-hire, specializing in system infiltration, data recovery, and counter-surveillance. Unlike most underground operators, she avoided chaos-for-profit. Her targets were corrupt syndicates, exploitative protocols, and shadow entities abusing the Kaspa network.

Her path crossed with Krex during an early Null Gang incursion. While Krex was tracking abnormal data flows, TESSA was already inside the compromised layer, isolating the breach. Instead of clashing, their operations synchronized.

Recognizing her precision and ethics, Krex offered her a place within his inner circle. TESSA accepted on one condition: she would remain autonomous, loyal to the mission, not to power.

Since then, TESSA has become the silent shield behind Kasparex operations. Where Krex leads openly and Vector builds infrastructure, TESSA works unseen, ensuring the network holds when pressure rises.
