# Style Guide – KREX

## Core Aesthetic
Neo-reptilian streetwear: sleek hoodie with glowing seams, layered tech-mesh, and angular panels.

## Color Palette
Primary: Neon Yellow (#FFD400), Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A)
Accents: Electric Cyan, Signal Orange

## Textures & Materials
Matte tech-mesh, reflective piping, scaled emboss on sleeves, softshell hood.

## Signature Gear
- **Neon Glasses** (holo-lens with subtle cyan glow)
- **Signal Hoodie** (glow seam + scale emboss)
- **Dataweave Gloves** (fingerless, conductive)

## Inspirations & Keywords
Cyberpunk, guardian, crisp geometry, crypto-sigil trims.
